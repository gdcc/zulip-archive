Hi @luddaniel , I can only answer your first question. DataCite will not automatically change the DOI status from "Findable" to "Registered." At Cirad Dataverse, we have some datasets where all versions are deaccessioned, and I had to manually change the DOI status in DataCite Fabrica for these specific datasets.

Dieuwertje Bloemen (Oct 14 2026 at 14:49):

Yeah, I think it's still the case, but haven't deaccessioned anything in a while. You can check it if you have a deaccessioned dataset by entering the doi in https://commons.datacite.org/, if it shows up without being logged in, then the doi is still set as "findable". If it is showing up, then we typically log into Fabrica, search for the doi and change their status in there to "registered".
