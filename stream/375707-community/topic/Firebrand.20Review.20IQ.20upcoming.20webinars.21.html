Less Spam, More Time for Research

How the Harvard Dataverse Repository uses ReviewIQ to support submission review

Register for a session

Spam and non-data deposits consume curator time and delay legitimate research. Join the Harvard Dataverse repository team and Firebrand Analytics for a practical, one-hour webinar on making submission review more manageable. See ReviewIQ in action, hear directly from the curators who use it, and learn what adoption could look like for your institution. Developed by Firebrand with Harvard’s Institute for Quantitative Social Science (IQSS) and the Harvard Dataverse Repository curation team, ReviewIQ has been running in the Harvard Dataverse Repository since August 2026. It screens submission metadata and returns a PASS, REVIEW, or BLOCK classification, typically within about five seconds, with a written explanation. Curators can inspect flagged submissions and correct classifications. Your team keeps the final call on flagged submissions. The current version does not open the research data files themselves. In a pre-launch evaluation of approximately 200 submissions, ReviewIQ’s classifications agreed with manual review 97% of the time.

What you’ll take away

•                     Harvard’s experience: Hear how the curation team is using ReviewIQ and what it has learned.

•                     A live view of the workflow: Follow a submission from screening through curator review, including the classification and its explanation.

•                     A clear picture of data handling and integration: Understand what information is sent, what comes back, and what your IT team would need to connect.

•                     A practical basis for deciding whether it fits: Discuss staff responsibilities, implementation, support, and cost. Bring your repository’s questions.

The 60-minute session includes a live demonstration and time for questions with the Harvard Dataverse and Firebrand teams.

Who should attend

Curators, repository managers, research data librarians, IT teams, and leaders responsible for research infrastructure at universities and nonprofit organizations.

Teams using other repository platforms are welcome. The demonstration will use Harvard Dataverse and discuss integration requirements for other environments.
