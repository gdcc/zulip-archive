Janvi Kapoor (Sep 24 2026 at 20:11):

Thanks for looping me in @Philip Durbin 🚀

Sharing my perspective as a student contributor:

When I initially applied to GSoC, my proposal didn’t make it. But staying in the community and continuing to contribute over time taught me that real open source isn't just about the seasonal application window. Understanding the codebase, familiarizing myself with maintainer workflows, and having patience to solve real issues (like getting PRs reviewed and merged after rejection) is what truly builds value.

Ash makes a fair point about the sudden influx of PRs during GSoC seasons which can overwhelm maintainers. At the same time, if structured with clear guidelines and dedicated mentors, it can introduce serious long-term contributors who stick around even after the program ends.

It definitely depends on whether the team has the bandwidth to mentor without pulling focus from core roadmap goals!
