Augustus Saunders (Oct 19 2026 at 20:18):

Hi everyone, I’m Augustus Saunders, founder and CTO of Firebrand. Thanks to Sonia and the team for making space for us here.

We’ve been working with Harvard Dataverse on ReviewIQ to help curators spend less time screening out spam and submissions that aren’t datasets. It’s been running in production since August, and Harvard estimates it’s giving its curation lead about eight hours back each week.

ReviewIQ uses deposit metadata and file listings to screen new submissions against the repository’s curation rules. It provides written reasons for its results, and curators make the final call on anything it holds for review. Deposits that pass the AI review continue and publish as they normally would.

It connects through Dataverse’s existing validation hooks, with no core changes, and runs in the repository’s own cloud. We can tune the screening rules to reflect how your team works.

We’re making ReviewIQ available to other installations as a commercial product. If this kind of screening is taking up more of your team’s time than you’d like, we’d be happy to share a short walkthrough and compare notes on where it could help.

Leo Andreev (Nov 09 2026 at 20:20):

Hi @Augustus Saunders, thank you again for for your work on this tool. It's been a fun project integrating it on our, HDV end.
For admins of other Dataverse instances, just wanted to expand on some things Augustus mentioned and give some quick pointers on how the tool integrates with Dataverse. (I do of course encourage you to attend the webinar above if you are potentially interested in integrating the tool with your instance).

It connects through Dataverse’s existing validation hooks, with no core changes, and runs in the repository’s own cloud.

As a reminder, the "existing validation hooks" are a very simple mechanism where an admin can configure Dataverse to call an arbitrary external script and pass to it a metadata export in our proprietary json format during the execution of the PublishDatasetCommand. Dataverse interprets the exit code of that executable as a binary pass or fail. If pass, it allows the publication to proceed. If fail, it shows the user a (configurable) message - along the lines of "the metadata did not pass our automated validation process; somebody will review your dataset shortly ..." and doesn't do anything else.

The Firebrand agent is an http-accessible service that accepts a metadata fragment in json and returns its judgement - whether it recommends to pass, review or block the publication, with lots of more granular additional information explaining the reasoning behind the decision, in both quantified and natural language forms.

At HDV we are using a fairly straightforward script that connects the 2 parts above together. I will make sure to clean it up and publish it, for other instances to use as a model. But just to be clear, please keep in mind that it will take you at least some DIY effort to port it to work within your environments. Some elements of my script are completely unique to the needs of our prod. instance. And I made no attempt to create a generic solution that could be used anywhere else "out of the box". As one example, in cases where blocking or extra reviewing is recommended by the AI, we have a system of notifying our curation team which relies on RT ticketing system - likely not something widely used within the community.

The cloud infrastructure (AWS in our case) where the agent runs was fairly straightforward to set up. I had to create a dedicated IAM user and give it a specified set of permissions in the AWS console. And ran a couple of Terraform scripts that their team supplied to create the required resources and services. You would need to make some integration decisions there as well, for example, how to authenticate your admin/curator users in the UI console they provide using your school or institution's identity services. But it should all be manageable.

I will post separately once I have my sample script checked in somewhere. And will do my best to answer any integration questions you may have.
