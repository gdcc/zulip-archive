Oliver Bertuch (Nov 17 2026 at 12:13):

There is no "reinstallation" when it comes to containers. There is only "drop the data and reindex".

Oliver Bertuch (Nov 17 2026 at 12:14):

Anytime you run Solr in a container, whatever has been installed in the container at runtime, will be executed

Oliver Bertuch (Nov 17 2026 at 12:14):

This saves you the burden of going through the installations steps yourself. A "reinstallation" roughly is equivalent to "pulling and runing a different image".

Oliver Bertuch (Nov 17 2026 at 12:15):

The important part is to ensure the data is at the correct locations, otherwise Solr doesn't know anything about it.

Oliver Bertuch (Nov 17 2026 at 12:17):

In case you are not sure which file on your disk is being mounted into the container at runtime (to hold your Solr core(s)), you can "inspect" (docker/podman inspect) the running container. That will give you a JSON you can comb through for the mounted volumes.

Oliver Bertuch (Nov 17 2026 at 12:17):

That way you can be 100% sure where your data is from / is going to.

Oliver Bertuch (Nov 17 2026 at 12:17):

All of this container business works very very very reliable and is quite battle tested. I highly suspect a mixup in your configuration.

Oliver Bertuch (Nov 17 2026 at 12:18):

As usual, mistakes in a configuration can only be found by pasting them...

Oliver Bertuch (Nov 17 2026 at 12:18):

Also, this is often a nice task for an AI. Feed it your compose file and the inspect JSON. It should be able to figure out if all of that is aligned.

Oliver Bertuch (Nov 17 2026 at 12:19):

In addition, log messages from the booting Solr container may provide helpful output.

Oliver Bertuch (Nov 17 2026 at 12:20):

I'd also recommend trying to start only the Solr bits on their own, to have less moving parts and less outputs to deal with.

Oliver Bertuch (Nov 17 2026 at 12:27):

As I don't know what the actual trouble is you are experiencing... Just in case... If you are using custom metadata blocks, make sure to add these to your Solr schema! Otherwise Dataverse will not be happy when comes dataset creation time.

Oliver Bertuch (Nov 17 2026 at 12:28):

I recommend using the Solr Driver Sidecar with solr-driver.sh, available as of 6.11 (can be backported)
